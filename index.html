About us

Our Story

Every idea deserves the right note.

THE8THNOTE is where it begins.

What started with a journey to Mumbai to learn, explore and express through music grew into a space where different sides of music could come together. From singing and piano to spiritual and bhakti music, THE8THNOTE is about discovering the many ways music can become a part of who we are.

Today, the vision is to build a musical community that goes beyond a page — a space where people can learn, express, explore and find their own note.

Meet Ronak Patiyat

Hi, I'm Ronak — a keyboardist and singer trained at one of Mumbai's leading music institutes. The 8th Note is my personalised, one-on-one way of teaching piano, harmonium and vocals: fun, interactive and creative, and built around what you actually want to play. I teach students 10 and above, in person in Bengaluru or live online — and I also perform devotional music at Jain and spiritual events.

  • Trained at a leading Mumbai music institute
  • Piano, harmonium & vocals — basic to advanced
  • 100+ Jain songs on piano & 100+ Jain stavans in voice
  • Bollywood piano and a classical vocal foundation (sur & taal)
  • In person at VV Puram, or live one-on-one online
