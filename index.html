About

Our Story

Every idea deserves the right note.

THE8THNOTE is where it begins.

What started with a journey to Mumbai to learn, explore and express through music grew into a space where different sides of music could come together. From singing and piano to spiritual and bhakti music, THE8THNOTE is about discovering the many ways music can become a part of who we are.

Today, the vision is to build a musical community that goes beyond a page — a space where people can learn, express, explore and find their own note.

    Meet Ronak Patiyat

    Hi, I'm Ronak — a keyboardist and singer trained at one of Mumbai's leading music institutes.
